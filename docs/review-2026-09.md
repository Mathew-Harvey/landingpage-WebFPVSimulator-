# Review, September 2026: simulator, landing page, board

A review of the three repositories on 2026-09-02, at the request of the owner,
in five parts: design and graphics, performance on an ordinary computer, game
play, bugs, and everything else. Findings are written down here whether or not
they are acted on, as the three CLAUDE.md files ask. Nothing in this document
has been changed in the code.

## What was covered, and what was not

Three code surveys completed: the simulator's core loop (`src/main.js`, boot,
loading, the frame path), its rendering (`src/render`, the quality presets,
the city pipeline join), and the shell plus accessibility, copy and sharing
across all five pages. Each survey drove the real pages in headless Chromium
and looked at its own screenshots, and each checked its findings against
CLAUDE.md and PROGRESS.md before reporting. Headless Chromium here renders on
SwiftShader, so every frame time below is relative; byte counts, mesh and
triangle counts, heap sizes, build times and DOM mutation counts are absolute.

Not covered, because the review ran out of session budget before those passes
ran: the game rules and flight feel (`src/game`, `src/input`, the plant), the
worlds (`src/maps`), audio and the share and publish flow, the track builder's
model, the landing film's own code and the wiki's figures, the board's server
and security, and a product level read of what would bring pilots back. Game
play is therefore the thinnest section here. Those passes can be run later.

Severity: high means fix before telling more people, medium is a clear
improvement, low is polish. Effort: small is under an hour, medium a day.

## 1. Design and graphics

1. **Target chevron lands on the stick gimbals and the phone centre stack.**
   Medium, small. `AIM_MARGIN_BOTTOM = 108` (`main.js:4269`) was sized for the
   corner blocks, but the keyboard stick ghost is about 124 px tall at the
   bottom centre and on touch the corner blocks move there too, so a gate
   below the frame, the normal case after takeoff, draws its chevron and range
   over the roll and pitch plate or over the speed readout. Seen in four
   flight captures at 1600x900 and 844x390. Raise the margin when the stick
   overlay is shown; on touch push a bottom band chevron to the nearer side.
2. **Low preset has no antialiasing at all.** Medium, small. The field's only
   antialiasing is the coverage term in the outline pass (`post.js:15-22`),
   and Low turns that pass off, so Low is a 0.85 scale frame with staircase
   edges on every pole and upright, then upscaled: the softest and the most
   aliased frame at once. The city ships an FXAA shader and Low turns that off
   too. Keep one FXAA pass on Low for both maps; it is cheaper than the 8 tap
   outline pass it replaces.
3. **Phone portrait title draws the bug chip over the wordmark.** Medium,
   small. At 430x932 the chip (x 190 to 414) covers the FPV letters of the
   wordmark (x 19 to 410). First thing a phone visitor sees. Below about 520
   px move the chip under the wordmark row or make it icon only.
4. **The command bar tells touch users to press arrow keys and Enter.**
   Medium, small. `legendFor` (`ui.js:7770-7790`) has a pad voice and a
   keyboard voice and no touch voice, although the shell knows it is on a
   touch screen because that is what mounts the thumb sticks. Add a third
   voice, "Tap a card", when `navigator.maxTouchPoints > 0` and no pad or
   keyboard has spoken.
5. **Nothing in flight or on the results screen is announced.** Medium,
   small. The only `aria-live` node in the shell is the music dock title
   (`ui.js:2374`). Banners, gate cues, lap times, pause and Run complete are
   written into plain divs and `show()` does not move focus. One polite live
   region fed by `setBanner` and `showResults`, and focus to the heading on
   `results` and `paused`.
6. **Landing: the closing headline sits under the fixed nav at the end of the
   scroll.** Medium, small. At 1440x900 and 1600x900 "You must practice." has
   its top at y 24 while the nav's bottom edge is at 66, so the film's last
   words are drawn behind WIKI, TRACK BUILDER, LEADERBOARD. At 430x932 the
   headline is above the viewport entirely. `#close` (`index.html:846-856`,
   `1224-1232`) needs the nav height reserved, or smaller cards under 1000 px
   of height so the close fits one screen.
7. **Landing: the first viewport shows no headline.** Low, small.
   `setCopy('assemble', T > 0.04 && T < 0.90)` (`src/main.js:1997`) hides the
   wordmark and both lede paragraphs until about a screen of scroll, so the
   page as it loads is a dark stage, a frame plate, the nav and SCROLL. On a
   phone that is the whole first impression. Not a recorded decision. Let the
   assemble copy show from T = 0.
8. **Phone flight OSD stacks six rows between the thumb plates.** Low, small.
   Under `.touch-fly-on` both corner blocks collapse to the bottom centre
   (`index.html:428-434`): PACK, volts and bar, km/h, mode, altitude, THROTTLE
   and bar in about 110 px of 13 px slate type over grass, with the chevron on
   top. Drop the two labels on touch and move the throttle bar onto the left
   plate's edge.
9. **PAUSED crumb and the music dock pill overprint at top left.** Low, small.
   Hide the dock while a titled screen is up, or clear its width.
10. **"Before you fly" hides the world.** Low, small. `worldLive`
    (`main.js:5138-5147`) omits `launch`, so between a title that shows the
    field and a flight that shows it again the card sits on flat black and
    reads as a load. Add `launch` to the attract condition.
11. **Field shadow camera follows the craft unsnapped.** Low, small. The city
    snaps its sun target to a 0.5 m grid with the comment that an exact
    follow shimmers at every texel boundary (`city/index.js:1899-1905`); the
    field copies the position exactly (`scene.js:5004-5008`), so every static
    shadow edge crawls as the quad moves. Five lines.
12. **Bloom threshold 0.78 sits on the horizon's luminance.** Low, small. The
    gate rings are about 0.70 luma and never bloom on their own; the fog and
    horizon colour is about 0.78, exactly on the threshold, so High carries a
    haze along the treeline that Medium does not. Try 0.82 (`post.js:411-422`)
    and re-run the pixels measurement.
13. **Hidden hints claim screen readers still find them.** Low, small.
    `.hint { display: none }` (`index.html:1862-1866`) removes all 14 hints
    from the accessibility tree. Delete them or use a visually hidden clip.
14. **Listbox holds heading divs; card buttons ignore Space.** Low, small.
    Headings are pushed into the `role=listbox` as plain divs
    (`ui.js:4569-4575`), and the `role=button` cards do not act on Space.
15. **maximum-scale=1 blocks pinch zoom on every menu for no gain.** Low,
    small, disputes the comment in `index.html:34-37` rather than a CLAUDE.md
    decision. iOS Safari has ignored it since iOS 10, and the flight surfaces
    already carry `touch-action: none`, which is what stops the double tap.
    Drop it and put `touch-action: manipulation` on the menu root.
16. **Contrast is strong throughout; one failure.** Low, small. Every token
    passes AAA on every house background (cream 14.5, sakura 8.9, amber 12.3,
    mint 13.9, slate 8.05 on deep). The calibration wizard's pending step
    labels, cream at 0.38 alpha and 11 px, are 3.1 to 1. Raise the alpha to
    about 0.55.
17. **Wiki favicon is hand drawn, off brand, and its fourth square is
    malformed.** Low, small. `wiki/index.html:45` inlines four pink squares
    whose last path closes to x = 9 and renders as a wedge. CLAUDE.md says the
    icon comes from the simulator's `scripts/icons.js`; point at `../icon.svg`.
18. **Known: the builder is unusable at 430 px, but phones are still sent
    there.** Low, small. PROGRESS records the phone layout as not done on
    purpose. What is not recorded: the board's Build a track button and the
    landing's card show it to phone visitors with no warning. A one line
    "built for a desktop and a mouse" note under 900 px until the decision
    is made.

## 2. Performance on an ordinary computer

1. **The race field has no pixel budget; High renders HiDPI laptops at 4 to 5
   Mpx.** High, small. `pixelRatioFor` is `min(dpr, cap) * scale` with cap 2 on
   High, and only the city block of `quality.js` has a `pixelBudget`
   (`quality.js:144, 154-172, 230-235`). A 1440x900 MacBook at DPR 2 renders
   2880x1800 through three full resolution passes plus grade and bloom into
   two RGBA16F targets: about 237 MB of render targets by scaling the measured
   900p ledger, against the project's own 120 MB ceiling, and 3.6x the fill of
   the 1080p frame the budget check pins. High's own note names "strong laptop
   iGPU (Intel Xe, M1)", which is exactly this class. Add a field
   `pixelBudget` around 2.6e6 and clamp the ratio so `w*h*pr*pr` stays under
   it. About 15 lines.
2. **Both render scale levers are broken.** High for the manual one, medium
   for the automatic one; small and medium. (a) `post.js:638-641` calls
   `composer.setSize` without `composer.setPixelRatio`, so after the pilot
   lowers Render scale the two HalfFloat composer targets, the colour pass,
   the 8 tap outline pass and the grade pass stay at boot size; only the
   prepass and the final blit shrink. Measured: slider at 55 left four passes
   at 1600x900 with the canvas at 880x495, and P5 fell only from 90.2 to 78.1
   MB; the outline reads an 880x495 buffer while drawing into 1600x900, so
   ink lines go blocky. One line. (b) In the city the slider is a no-op:
   `CityPipeline` captures the shell's pixel ratio at construction and
   restores it on every resize (`city/index.js:458, 469-475, 491`), and
   `internalScale` says scale 1 where the pipeline renders 1.34x. (c) The
   automatic pacer never runs: `main.js` only calls it when a map's post
   object has `applyPace`, and no map defines one since the compact maps were
   removed, so `pace.js` and the promise in `quality.js:36-40` describe
   something the product does not do. Wire `applyPace` into both pipelines or
   delete the pacer and its two comments.
3. **Auto detection gives High to the 2020 iGPU laptop that Medium is written
   for.** Medium, small. `detectDefaultGraphics` returns Low for Steam Deck,
   phones and iPads and High for everything else (`quality.js:198-220`), yet
   the file says Medium is sized for UHD 620, Iris Plus and MX350. The boot
   path already reads the GPU name and lowers a detected preset for software
   rasterisers (`main.js:422-448`); extend that branch with a conservative
   name test (Intel HD, UHD, Iris, Radeon Graphics, Vega, Mali, Adreno) that
   lowers a detected value to Medium. This touches the "default to High"
   comment but not its reasons, and PROGRESS records no measurement of any
   map on an Iris Xe class part.
4. **The city supersamples 1.34x at 900p and 1.5x on 1366x768, on Medium
   too.** Medium, small, disputes a recorded decision in part. `preferScale`
   is null on High and Medium so the vendored 1.5x rule applies
   (`quality.js:133, 166`, `city/index.js:469-475`). On the commonest laptop
   panel High draws 2.36 Mpx on a 1.05 Mpx screen through a HalfFloat target,
   depth, ink, grade and FXAA, and Medium draws 1.21x. The recorded choice
   (`city/index.js:1817-1834`) was made at 1080p for High and does not cover
   Medium or small panels. `preferScale 1` on Medium; cap the High fallback at
   1.12 below 1080p.
5. **The boot fetch chain is fully serial, and it waits on the sleeping
   board.** Medium, small. three.js, then main.js and its 40 module graph,
   then the board round trips, then the wasm, then the tune diff, then the
   map's modules, then the world build: five or six dependent round trips
   before anything is built. Measured: `dist/sim.wasm` first requested at
   1519 ms, after both board calls (1394 to 1513 ms). Add `<link rel=preload>`
   for the wasm and the default diff and `modulepreload` for `src/main.js`,
   `src/ui/ui.js` and the default map entry to `index.html`, and start the
   wasm fetch alongside the board fetch (see bug 1).
6. **The OSD rewrites the DOM every frame with no dirty check.** Medium,
   small. `setOsd` (`ui.js:7309-7425`) assigns `textContent` and `className`
   to about a dozen elements every frame, `setBanner` writes three
   properties every frame, and `syncAngleMode` writes the Settings craft
   caption from the frame loop on every screen. Measured with a
   MutationObserver in flight: 17 mutation records per frame, 3 per frame on
   the title. Assigning an identical string still replaces the Text node and
   invalidates style on an overlay composited above WebGL, which is the case
   an integrated GPU pays for most. `scorehud.js:148-183` already has the
   guarded pattern with the reason written down; apply it, and drive the two
   bars with `transform: scaleX`.
7. **Terrain and outline hulls are 44 percent of the field's triangles and
   never culled.** Medium, medium. The terrain is one 1700 m plane of 105,800
   triangles with a 1202 m bounding radius, drawn in the colour pass and again
   in the prepass; the merged inverted hull bucket is 101,840 triangles at a
   526 m radius, also drawn twice. About 415k of 949k triangles per frame on
   High, and half of Low's 393k. Split the terrain into an 8x8 grid of chunks
   and bake hulls per spatial cell like the city's `MERGE_CELL`, so the
   renderer culls what is behind the camera. PROGRESS notes P2 has failed at
   about 1.6x for five rounds; this is where the reachable saving is.
8. **`readState` allocates a fresh Float64Array on every 1 ms step.** Medium,
   medium. `tests/lib/simmod.js:190-200` copies the state block into a new
   typed array and wrapper per call, and `main.js` calls it per physics step
   (`4675`), per contact, per frame while landed and per flight frame: at
   least a thousand allocations a second in steady flight and 100 to 200
   inside one catch-up frame. Keep a ring of three preallocated buffers for
   the shell path; `statePrev`, `stateCurr` and `stNow` are compared by
   identity so a double buffer is not enough.
9. **Catch-up frames pay heavy per step JS; a slow laptop gets slow motion.**
   Medium, medium. dt is capped at 100 ms (`main.js:4411`), so a slow frame
   steps up to 100 plant steps, each with a height query and `sim_set_ground`,
   `sim.step(1)`, a state copy, `sim_ground_contacts`, a ground normal and an
   obstacle sweep. Once a frame costs more than 100 ms the sim advances less
   than wall time and the whole game runs slow, with the lap clock still
   honest, so a slow laptop silently flies a slow motion lap. Batch steps
   between contact passes, raise ground only near the surface, publish steps
   per frame in the F3 readout, and consider pausing with a notice when
   consecutive frames hit the cap.
10. **The city compiles 137 shader programs on its first drawn frame.**
    Medium, small. The field precompiles (`scene.js:4987`); the city does
    not, and ends its load with 137 live programs against the field's 48. The
    "frame" loading stage measured 4231 ms for the city against 1611 ms for
    the field, relative. Call `renderer.compileAsync(scene, camera)` as a
    loading stage before "frame" so the bar covers it.
11. **The city sign atlas is 30 Mpx of RGBA8 without mips, about 122 MB.**
    Medium, medium. Seven 4096 sheets, 30,445,712 sheet pixels, `generateMipmaps
    false` (`bake.js:746-756`), on top of 70 other textures, on the map that
    already carries the largest target ledger. PROGRESS recorded 24.5 Mpx on
    five sheets when the atlas landed, so it has grown 24 percent. Rasterise
    the sign canvases at half resolution before packing, or use power of two
    tiles with padded borders so mips can come back.
12. **Resize is not coalesced.** Low, small. Every resize event reallocates
    the composer's targets and the bloom ladder (`main.js:519-528`,
    `post.js:637-652`). Set a flag and apply once at the top of `frame()`.
13. **Pixel ratio is fixed at boot.** Low, small. Browser zoom and moving the
    window between a 2x and a 1x monitor fire resize but never re-run
    `applyPixelRatio` (`shell.js:216-230`, `main.js:519-528`). Compare and
    reapply in the resize handler; land it after item 2(a) or the composer
    will not follow.
14. **Landed and turtle frames re-pin the RC grid every frame.** Low, small.
    `adoptSimClock` runs every frame on the pad, copying state and reseeding
    the link's jitter stream 60 times a second (`main.js:4855, 2213`). Call it
    on transitions only, as its comment describes.
15. **The pace controller keeps the old map's EMA across a swap.** Low,
    small. Add a `reset()` to `pace.js` and call it from `adoptLoadedView`.

Not measured here, because that pass did not run: what a title screen visit
downloads from the 81 MB of `assets/music`, the city's module waterfall, cache
headers and compression on production, and timings on a throttled network.

## 3. Game play

Only the core loop was surveyed for play, so this section covers the first
sixty seconds and nothing about racing rules, scoring or flight feel.

1. **Throttle during the intro takes off while the camera is still a third
   person dolly.** Medium, small. The takeoff branch runs regardless of the
   intro, and throttle only skips the intro to `INTRO_FLY`, so a pilot who
   throttles up during the pad shot leaves the ground while the camera spends
   the next second dollying into the FPV lens: flying a quad they cannot see
   from, with their own props across the frame. Measured airborne at 2.87 m
   mid zoom. On the takeoff edge snap to the FPV pose, or keep `landed`
   latched until the zoom completes (`main.js:4495-4520, 5285-5290`).
2. **The intro clock caps at 33 ms per frame, so under 30 fps it plays in slow
   motion.** Medium, small. `INTRO_STEP_MAX = 33` (`main.js:213`) was meant to
   stop one hitch burning the pad shot, but it caps steady state too: at 25
   fps the 4 s intro takes 4.8 s, at 20 fps 6.1 s, on every run start and
   every Restart run. Measured at about 9 fps the intro ran at 0.3x. Advance
   by `min(dt, 100)` like the physics accumulator.
3. **"Restart run" replays the 4 s pad shot; R does not.** Low, small. The
   menu path sets `introMs = 0` (`main.js:3471-3478`); the key does not. A
   racer restarting dozens of times an hour pays 4 s from the menu, which is
   the only path a phone has. Treat restart like R.
4. **A hidden tab does not pause.** Low, small. `visibilitychange` only mutes
   the mix (`main.js:5755-5765`); the pilot who alt-tabs returns to a live quad
   resuming at speed with 100 ms of stick history in the first frame. Run the
   same two calls Escape makes when the document hides in flight.

## 4. Bugs

1. **Boot awaits the sleeping board before fetching the flight controller.**
   High, small. `main.js:474-479` await the share and most flown track
   adopters before `loading.start('sim')` at `:574`. The `/api/tracks` list
   has a 4 s timeout (`board.js:301`) but `fetchTrackDocument`, used by both
   adopters and by every Fly link from the board, has none, so a board that
   accepts and hangs hangs boot forever. DEPLOY.md records that the free
   board sleeps and takes about a minute to wake, then says the simulator is
   unaffected; this code makes that untrue. While it waits the loading label
   still points at the renderer stage and blames the CDN. Start the wasm
   fetch concurrently, give the document fetch a timeout, and add a board
   stage or move adoption after `sim.init`.
2. **The Render scale slider leaves the field's passes at full size, and is a
   no-op in the city.** High, small. See performance item 2; recorded here
   because the setting does not do what its label says.
3. **The automatic render scale pacer is dead code.** Medium, medium. See
   performance item 2(c).
4. **Launch card opens with focus on Radio link, so Enter opens a dropdown,
   not Fly.** Medium, small, medium confidence. The card's hint says "Enter
   flies it", the Fly row is marked primary (`ui.js:4124-4134`), but in three
   headless runs Race then Enter landed with the focus bar on the Radio link
   row and Enter opened its dropdown; mode stayed title through further
   Enters. Set the menu index to the primary row on `show('launch')`, and let
   Enter on an info row fall through to the primary action. The harness
   synthesises keydown, so confirm on a real keyboard first.
5. **No error boundary in `frame()`.** Medium, small. The next frame is
   scheduled first (`main.js:4402`) and `readState`, `sim_set_pose` and map
   queries can throw, so any fault leaves the picture frozen, the OSD stale
   and one identical error per frame in the console, with nothing on screen
   for the pilot. One try/catch that shows a banner and resets or stops.
6. **Medium and Low notes promise thinned planting; the field has no planting
   lever.** Medium, small. The Settings notes (`quality.js:74, 112, 142`) say
   "thinned planting" and "fewer plants", but the field reads only shadow
   settings: measured 949,309 triangles on High and 949,296 on Medium, 134
   meshes on all three presets. Only the city thins. Reword, or add a field
   lever without moving the rng stream.
7. **README verify section is stale.** Medium, small. `README.md:90-93` says
   13 checks and 12 pass with yaw-coupling red; PROGRESS.md's latest entry
   records 15 of 15 with one SKIP, and `verify.js` documents a SKIP state.
8. **README keys M, C and V are bound to nothing.** Medium, small.
   `README.md:40, 47-48`. No file under `src` binds KeyM, KeyC or KeyV;
   `main.js` handles R, X and L, and calibration is reached through the
   choose pad screen. PROGRESS.md:311 already noted M was unbound.
9. **Dead `boot()` call at the foot of `main.js` fails on every load.** Low,
   small. `main.js:6893` calls `boot()` with no argument at module scope; it
   throws, appends a "could not start" banner, and is hidden only because
   `Ui.build()` wipes `#ui` a microtask later. Delete lines 6893 to 6899;
   `boot.js` owns the entry.
10. **OSD altitude queries the height field unbiased.** Low, small.
    `main.js:5579` uses `p.y` where every contact query uses `y -
    SURFACE_BIAS`, so under the overbridge deck the readout can pick the deck
    and print a negative altitude. Same omission in `__craftState`.
11. **Keys reach the shell during a map swap.** Low, small, low confidence.
    `syncWorld` disposes the old world before `loadMap` resolves and
    `handleKey` has no loading guard, so Enter on the title mid swap runs
    `reset()` against a disposed view. Self heals; refuse shell keys while a
    swap is in flight.

## 5. Anything else

1. **Twelve shipped music tracks have no recorded author or licence.**
   Medium, small. `assets/music` holds twelve titles as mp3 and webm, served
   immutable and played in menus and flight. `tracks.js` records id and name
   only, NOTICE has no audio section, and the PROGRESS entry that introduced
   them names no composer. The repository is GPLv3 and redistributes them, so
   the terms need writing down, and if they are not GPL compatible the files
   need excluding from the grant. A Music section in NOTICE and a line on the
   Credits screen.
2. **No sitemap anywhere; the production robots.txt is Cloudflare's stub.**
   Medium, small. None of the three repositories ships `robots.txt` or
   `sitemap.xml` and the worker does not synthesise one. Search engines
   discover the five pages only by links and the wiki articles not at all.
   Add both to the landing repository root, which is what the worker serves
   at `/`.
3. **All 36 wiki articles share one tab title and one indexable URL.**
   Medium, small. `wiki.js:372-386` pushes history per article but never sets
   `document.title`, so every article, history entry and bookmark reads "FPV
   wiki, WebFPV". Set the title and description where the hash is pushed.
4. **The builder and the wiki lack the share card the other pages carry.**
   Medium, small. `src/trackbuilder/index.html:31-46` has no description,
   canonical or og tags; `wiki/index.html:35-45` has a description only. The
   landing also has two h1 elements. Copy the head block from the simulator.
5. **Six files ship without the GPL header the conventions require.** Low,
   small. Board `scripts/seed.js`, `schema.sql`, `render.yaml`,
   `docker-compose.yml`; simulator `render.yaml` and
   `.claude/workflows/fpv-judge-loop.js`.

## Method

Findings came from three review agents, each reading one subsystem and driving
the pages in headless Chromium, each told to re-open its cited lines and grep
CLAUDE.md and PROGRESS.md before reporting, and to label anything recorded as
known or deliberate. Screenshots and measurement scripts were kept outside the
repositories and are not committed.
