01 · The machine

WEBFPV

A five inch racing quad, built in front of you. Frame, stack, motors, loom, camera, props.

Then it flies on Betaflight 4.5.1, compiled to WebAssembly and running the real control loop at 1 kHz. Not an approximation of one.

03 · The line

Now fly it.

Camera on the airframe, thirty degrees up, eighty five degree lens. Every gate is a decision made three gates early.

04 · The reason

The hard part of FPV
is getting to the first lap.

A quad, a radio, goggles, batteries, a charger. Hundreds of dollars and a month of crashing before any of it is fun. The simulators that fix that are good, and they are still a purchase, a download, and a PC to install them on.

So I built one that asks for nothing. A tab, and the pad already on your desk.

05 · The point

Don’t be a
Sh#t pilot. You must practice.
