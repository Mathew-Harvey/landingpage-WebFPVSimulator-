01 · The machine

WEBFPV

A five inch racing quad, built in front of you. Frame, stack, motors, loom, camera, props.

Then it flies on Betaflight 4.5.1, compiled to WebAssembly and running the real control loop at 1 kHz. Not an approximation of one.
