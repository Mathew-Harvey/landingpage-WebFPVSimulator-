Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

I-term relax

CLI setting iterm_relax. Works here. Parameter group PID_PROFILE. Firmware type UINT8. Values: OFF, RP, RPY, RP_INC, RPY_INC.

What it does

Stops the I term growing during a fast stick movement, so that a flip does not build up a correction that pushes the quad on when you stop. The options are OFF, RP (roll and pitch), RPY (roll, pitch and yaw), and RP_INC and RPY_INC, which hold I back only while it would be growing.

How it works

The options are the itermRelax_e values in pid.h. Betaflight passes the setpoint, the target rate, through a high-pass filter, which keeps only its fast changes. The faster the setpoint is changing, the more the I term is held back. iterm_relax_type chooses how the held-back amount is worked out.

In this simulator

Works here, in Betaflight's compiled pid.c.

If you raise it

This is a list of options, not a number. Moving from RP to RPY adds yaw to the relaxed axes. The I term builds up less during fast movements, so it also corrects less while they last.

RPY includes the yaw axis. The _INC options hold I back less, because they still let the I term shrink during a movement.

If you lower it

With OFF, the I term builds up during a flip and works against it, then pushes the quad on when you stop.

OFF was the default in older versions of Betaflight, and it is a common reason for a quad to twitch at the end of a roll.
