Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

Yaw spin recovery

CLI setting yaw_spin_recovery. Works here. Parameter group GYRO_CONFIG. Firmware type UINT8. Values: OFF, ON, AUTO.

What it does

Stops an uncontrolled yaw spin, the kind that can follow a hard hit. When the gyro reads a yaw rate above the threshold, Betaflight sets the yaw target to zero, clears the I term on every axis, switches off P, D and feedforward on roll and pitch, and lets yaw P use the whole motor range to stop the spin. It ends once the yaw rate has stayed 100 degrees per second below the threshold for 20 ms.

How it works

The detection is in sensors/gyro.c and the response in pid.c and mixer.c, all compiled into the simulator.

In this simulator

Works here. The physics model will spin the quad as fast as the motors can, and this adds no damping of its own: it only changes what the controller does.

The choices

AUTO: the threshold is worked out from your maximum yaw rate, plus a quarter of it or 200 degrees per second, whichever is more. yaw_spin_threshold is not used. This is Betaflight's default.

ON: the threshold is yaw_spin_threshold.

OFF: Betaflight never steps in.
