Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

Throttle limit type

CLI setting throttle_limit_type. Works here. Parameter group CONTROL_RATE_PROFILES. Firmware type UINT8. Values: OFF, SCALE, CLIP.

What it does

OFF, SCALE or CLIP. SCALE spreads the whole stick travel from zero up to the cap, which is how racers move hover higher up the stick on a quad with far more thrust than it needs to hover. CLIP ignores the top of the stick.

How it works

applyThrottleLimit in mixer.c. SCALE: output = stick × cap. CLIP: output = the smaller of stick and cap.

In this simulator

Works here. The Throttle limit row on the Rates menu in Settings writes SCALE when the cap is below 100 and OFF at 100, so CLIP cannot be chosen there.

If you raise it

This is a choice from a list, so there is no higher or lower value. SCALE is the type that gives back fine control of the throttle.

SCALE multiplies the throttle by the cap, so the full stick travel covers a smaller throttle range and each part of the stick changes the throttle less.

If you lower it

OFF gives the five inch its full thrust, about 8 times its weight under Earth's gravity and about 5 times at the normal Weight setting. CLIP is a poor choice: you lose the top of the stick and keep the touchy bottom part.

OFF applies no limit. CLIP cuts the throttle off at the cap and leaves the stick below it unchanged.
