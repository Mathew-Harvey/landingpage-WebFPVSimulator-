Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

Roll RC rate (centre)

CLI setting roll_rc_rate. Works here. Parameter group CONTROL_RATE_PROFILES. Firmware type UINT8. Range 1 to 255.

What it does

On ACTUAL rates this is the roll centre sensitivity, in tens of deg/s: how fast the rotation rate rises as the stick leaves the centre. A value of 7 means 70 deg/s for each unit of stick movement at the centre. It is the slope of the curve at the middle, not the rate at half stick.

How it works

Stored in rcRates[FD_ROLL] as a whole number up to 255. On ACTUAL, centre sensitivity = rc_rate × 10. The other types use the number differently: see applyBetaflightRates and the other rate functions in fc/rc.c.

In this simulator

Works here. It is set on the Rates menu in Settings. Roll and pitch share one value unless you turn on Separate pitch, and yaw has its own.

If you raise it

The quad's roll becomes twitchier near the centre of the stick, and fine aiming gets harder.

On ACTUAL, the straight-line part of the curve near the centre becomes steeper.

If you lower it

The centre feels softer. Small corrections need larger movements of the right stick to the left and right.

The slope at the centre is shallower. On ACTUAL the maximum rate is still set by srate.
