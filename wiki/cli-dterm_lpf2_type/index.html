Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

D term low-pass 2 type

CLI setting dterm_lpf2_type. Works here. Parameter group PID_PROFILE. Firmware type UINT8. Values: PT1, BIQUAD, PT2, PT3.

What it does

Chooses the kind of filter used for low-pass filter 2 on the D term. A low-pass filter lets slow changes through and removes fast ones. The kinds differ in how sharply they cut above the cutoff frequency and how much delay they add below it.

How it works

The kinds come from Betaflight's filter.c. PT1 is a first-order filter. PT2 and PT3 are two and three first-order stages in a row, each set a little higher so that the whole filter still cuts at the frequency you choose. BIQUAD is a second-order filter with a sharper bend at the cutoff. The cutoff frequency is a separate setting.

In this simulator

Works here, in Betaflight's compiled pid_init.c and pid.c.

The choices

PT1: the gentlest cut, and the least delay for its cutoff.

BIQUAD: a second-order filter, like PT2 but with a sharper bend at the cutoff.

PT2: two first-order stages. It removes more above the cutoff than PT1, and adds more delay.

PT3: three first-order stages. It removes more again, and adds more delay again.
