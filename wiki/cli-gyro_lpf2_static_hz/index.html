Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

Gyro low-pass 2 (Hz)

CLI setting gyro_lpf2_static_hz. Works here. Parameter group GYRO_CONFIG. Firmware type UINT16. Range 0 to 1000 Hz.

What it does

The cutoff frequency, in hertz, of low-pass filter 2 on the gyro signal. A low-pass filter lets changes slower than its cutoff through and removes faster ones, and P, I, D and everything else that reads the gyro see the result. 0 turns the filter off.

How it works

Filter 2 has no dynamic version, so this is its cutoff whenever it is above 0. A real flight controller reads its gyro thousands of times a second. This simulator reads it 1,000 times a second, so nothing above 500 Hz reaches the filter here.

In this simulator

Works here, in Betaflight's compiled gyro_init.c and gyro.c. The vibration it removes is added to the simulated gyro reading, not to the quad's motion.

If you raise it

Less smoothing and less delay. The quad feels more directly connected to the sticks, and more noise gets through to the motors.

A higher cutoff lets faster changes through. Judge it in a full throttle climb, where the vibration is strongest, not in a hover.

If you lower it

More smoothing and more delay. The quad feels softer, and too low a cutoff makes it slow to respond to everything.

A lower cutoff removes more noise and adds delay to the loop, and delay in a feedback loop brings it closer to oscillating.
