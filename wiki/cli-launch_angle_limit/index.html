Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

Launch angle limit

CLI setting launch_angle_limit. Works here. Parameter group PID_PROFILE. Firmware type UINT8. Range 0 to 80.

What it does

Sets how far the quad may tilt forward, in degrees, while launch control holds it on the start line. 0 means no limit.

How it works

In Betaflight's code this is launchControlAngleLimit, in degrees. When you pitch forward during the hold, Betaflight slows the pitch movement over the last 10 degrees before the limit and stops it at the limit. Betaflight uses the limit only when it has an accelerometer; the simulator gives it one, using the attitude from the physics model.

In this simulator

Works here. The simulator sets it to 40 degrees by default, so that the quad does not tip far enough forward to loop over when you punch. Betaflight's own default is 0. A tune or the firmware bench can change it.

If you raise it

You can tilt the quad further forward on the line before you launch.

A larger limit allows a larger forward pitch angle before Betaflight stops the pitch movement.

If you lower it

The quad cannot tilt as far forward. The angle is measured from level, so on a launch stand that is not level the limit may stop the quad sooner or later than you expect.

A smaller limit stops the forward pitch at a smaller angle. At 0 there is no limit at all.
