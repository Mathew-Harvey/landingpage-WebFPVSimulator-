Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

DShot idle

CLI setting dshot_idle_value. Works here. Parameter group MOTOR_CONFIG. Firmware type UINT16. Range 0 to 2000.

What it does

The lowest power the mixer will send to a motor, in DShot units: percent times 100, so 550 is 5.5 percent. Too low, and yaw stops working at idle. Too high, and the quad will not come down.

How it works

Stored as digitalIdleOffsetValue. motorInitEndpoints in bf_glue.c, the code that connects Betaflight to the physics, sets the motor range from DShot values, not from the PWM setting min_throttle.

In this simulator

Works here. This is the idle that airmode relies on.

If you raise it

The motors spin faster at idle. The quad floats more at low throttle, yaw is stronger at the bottom of the throttle, and the quad is harder to bring down.

A higher digital idle raises the lowest power a motor can receive.

If you lower it

The motors come closer to stopping at idle. Drops are quicker and yaw feels weak at the bottom. On a real electronic speed controller (ESC), a very low idle risks a desync, where the ESC loses track of the motor; that is not modelled.

A lower idle lowers the lowest power a motor can receive. The physics model spins the motor at whatever power it is given and cannot desync.
