Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

Crashflip motor percent

CLI setting crashflip_motor_percent. Works here. Parameter group MIXER_CONFIG. Firmware type UINT8. Range 0 to 100 %.

What it does

Sets how much power turtle mode gives to the motors that are not doing the flip. Turtle mode, which Betaflight calls crashflip, spins the motors on one side of an upside-down quad to roll it back over. At 0 the other motors stay stopped.

How it works

Betaflight's turtle code, applyFlipOverAfterCrashModeToMotors in mixer.c, turns the pitch and roll stick positions into a flip power and sends it to the motors on one side. The motors on the other side receive crashflip_motor_percent of that power, or nothing when the setting is 0.

In this simulator

Works here. Hold the T key and Betaflight's turtle mode takes over the mixer: the pitch and roll sticks spin the motors on one side, and this setting is read. The automatic flip that plays when the quad comes to rest upside down is separate and does not read it. DShot reverse, which runs the motors backwards in turtle mode on a real quad, is not modelled.

If you raise it

With T held, the motors on the other side spin as well, faster the higher you set it.

Those motors get a larger share of the flip power. At 100 they receive the same power as the motors doing the flip.

If you lower it

The other motors spin more slowly in turtle mode, and at 0 they stay stopped.

At 0, Betaflight gives the other motors no power, so only the motors on one side turn.
