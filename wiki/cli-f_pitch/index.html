Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

Feedforward pitch

CLI setting f_pitch. Works here. Parameter group PID_PROFILE. Firmware type UINT16. Range 0 to 1000.

What it does

Feedforward on pitch starts the motors as soon as you move the right stick forward and back, before the gyro has measured any error. It makes the quad feel closely connected to the stick. It is also the first thing uneven radio timing makes rough.

How it works

F is a gain on the rate of change of the setpoint, after averaging, smoothing, jitter reduction, boost and the maximum rate limit. It does not use the error. A perfect 250 Hz radio link makes this rate of change smoother than ExpressLRS does.

In this simulator

Works here. It sets the F gain in Betaflight's PID profile. If you fly ExpressLRS, tune F on the ELRS 250 Hz radio link preset, or the settings you export will be too twitchy.

If you raise it

Pitch responds ahead of the error. Too much makes the quad overshoot, especially at the start of a movement.

A higher F leaves less of the stick tracking to P and can hide a low P on a graph. It does not help the quad recover from a disturbance, and radio jitter becomes motor activity.

If you lower it

You wait a moment after moving the stick before the quad responds.

The delay in following the stick is left to P and to the motors' own delay. Many pilots fly like this.
