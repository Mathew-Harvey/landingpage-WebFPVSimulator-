Betaflight 4.5.1 settings reference

TPA rate

CLI setting tpa_rate. Works here. Parameter group PID_PROFILE. Firmware type UINT8. Range 0 to 100.

What it does

How much TPA (throttle PID attenuation) reduces the gains above the breakpoint, as a percentage. The reduction grows in a straight line from nothing at tpa_breakpoint to this percentage at full throttle. 0 is off, and 70 is a large reduction.

How it works

tpa_rate is a whole number from 0 to 100. pid_init.c turns it into a slope, so that at full throttle the gains are multiplied by 1 − tpa_rate / 100.

In this simulator

Works here. The physics model's thrust depends strongly on the throttle, and at full throttle the five inch makes about 5 times its weight in thrust at the normal Weight setting (about 8.1 times at Earth gravity), so TPA has a real effect here.

If you raise it

The quad feels softer and more stable during full-throttle bursts. It also has less control authority at the top of the throttle.

A higher rate reduces the gains more at high throttle.

If you lower it

The gains tuned for the hover stay at full strength all the way to full throttle, which often makes the quad buzz there.

A rate of 0 turns TPA off.
