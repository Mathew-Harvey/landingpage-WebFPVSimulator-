22 September

16 to 22 September

New

  • Patreon is under the WebFPV name on the title screen, and in the bar on this site. Keep the lights on is $3, Hosting + runway is $8, Build the sim is $20. US dollars a month.
  • There is a weight slider on the flight screen. Right is heavier, so the quad drops when you chop the throttle. Left is lighter and it hangs. Normal sits at ninety percent of full Sinky, which is where flying it to the stop felt about right, with room either side. The old slider changed drag, how far the quad carries, and a lap line could print "air at undefined percent".
  • Rates are saved in this browser, throttle cap included, and you can keep more than one set. Open them from pause. Changing rates in the air used to restart the run and drop you on the ground.
  • Stick mode is Mode 1 and Mode 2, on a radio, a game controller, or the keyboard.
  • Tracks and Times has a Statistics tab. Crashes are in it. A crashflip is left out of the flight time.

The town

  • Gaps that looked open were solid. A ceiling was missing between two buildings, a hip roof had no peak, and a roof deck you could see was a lid.
  • The bridge deck had a slot in it. The canal was solid. The mountains were hollow and the bore had no roof. Four bushes were sitting in the air on a crest. The cafe tables were 1.6 m past the rail.
  • Hit a wall and you bounced between two spots until you restarted. The props were pushing you into the face.

Sticks

  • A game controller's throttle springs back to centre. The sim read that spring as a punch, and the quad took off by itself.
  • The calibration wizard says what it is waiting for. You can finish it with the throttle held down, and a four-axis radio can finish it. A three-position switch is no longer asked to return to a centre it does not have. A gimbal that rests a little off centre keeps that rest, so the quad does not creep with the sticks let go.
  • One channel ran backwards, the sticks were on the wrong hands, and a stick stayed down after you let go. The camera angle slider was moving the quad.

Fixes

  • The settings room is called Settings. The menus were sending you to a calibrate screen that was not there.
  • Menu rows slid out from under the pointer. The help note was changing the height of the row.
  • A frozen picture says what it is waiting on.
  • The board's crash count read zero. It only heard the clip-through catch, and missed a real hit. Race and freestyle now use the same line, and a turtle after that hit is not counted again. A visit's country was coming through as Unknown.
  • The empty-board sentence was showing up when the board had failed to load, tracks and all.
  • A rate preset with no id showed in the list and could not be loaded or deleted.
  • A feel report now records the sticks as well as the tune. Five reports that said twitchy, stiff, floppy or soft were two phones, a keyboard whose held key only reached about a third of the stick, and a RadioMaster Boxer.
  • Tune is one list, in the Quad room. Two old tunes came off it, and the aircraft picker is in that room.
  • The flight music lost three tracks and gained five. The wiki was saying the same thing twice on a page.

Held back

  • The trick list showed one trick, the wheel did nothing, and the panel stuck on the first row. It was also naming tricks wrong. Both doors are gone, the one on the title and the one in the Freestyle room, until a flown trick gets the name you flew.

15 September

13 to 15 September

New

  • The whoop has its own motors, weight and camera, and it parks about a centimetre off the floor. The screens had been calling it by the five inch's name.
  • Whoop racing started as four rooms, then the RaceGOW Season 5 tracks went in, each named for the person who drew it. A room card plays the lap. Track 6 is MrE's, the first with no sill. Track 7 is FPVBean's.
  • Dive gates in a whoop room stand on a short pipe. You can fly a gap in the frame.
  • The board names the person who built the track, and you can search for them. The mark in the corner of the board goes back to this site.

Fixes

  • A whoop room could not be finished. The scoring square on a pole was in the author's metres, so a clean pass did not count.
  • Tracks 1, 3 and 4 on the board were still the old layouts. They were replaced in place and the times stayed. Track 1 had also been mirrored, so the lap ran the wrong way round.
  • Brushing the floor no longer fails a gate. Being on your side does. The bottom third of a whoop gate was marked dirt and refused.
  • The whoop's hit box hung under it, canopy and all, because the body size was read as a radius. It is a width. The ducts are drawn, and a pole's foot is solid.
  • Gyro noise was scaled to a five inch motor. In a hover the whoop buzzed several times harder than the big quad at full throttle, and the floor was handing it free lift.
  • The whoop was on the five inch's tune, in a room that had grown to fit the big quad. The tune follows the machine. The card says which aircraft you are on.
  • Posting a time still finds the track if the board has given it a new id.
  • The racing line was going through a gate it was not scoring, and a steering rule was pushing it off Track 7. Tracks 3 and 4 were retuned off the animations.
  • A gate stands up or it lies flat. One build had added a third pose, half tilted, that no track uses.

8 September

8 to 12 September

New

  • The Track room lists the board and the tracks in this browser, shipped rooms included. A card plays the lap from a three-quarter view at one pace, so a longer lap is a longer clip.
  • More, in the builder, writes a looping animation of the lap. The racing line can close on the first gate, with the start pads left out of it.
  • Whoop gates are 28 inches. Tracks 5 and 8 were rebuilt to that. Six guessed tracks, which were never the real rooms, came out.
  • Back is a row, so the corner is clear. The name on the music control is the mute. The bug button is on every screen except the title. A rename of that button had left it dead anywhere a browser still had yesterday's script.
  • The film's indoor lap is RaceGOW Track 1. The five inch on the front page reads 6S, which is what the instrument says. The stickers are slapped on.

Fixes

  • The racing line was going wide around a stack and through gates that were not in the order.
  • The loading bar sat at fifteen percent, then jumped to done. Building the world is most of the wait, and the bar had nothing to report for it.
  • Whoop rooms had floor paint and gate numbers. Those are gone.
  • An admin can take a track off the board. The whoop list is named for the aircraft.

1 September

30 August to 7 September

New

  • Freestyle scoring has three positions, and Off is the one you get until you pick another. Free flight names the tricks and posts nothing. A two minute run can go to the board. The row says the scorer is unfinished.
  • There is a training field north of the works, five skills, and the bars are solid. They had been drawn and then never made it into the collision.
  • Freestyle is the town. The front page flies that town and ends over it. It had been a painting of the town, and a bug was moving the real one twice, which ate the shops.
  • The board has a freestyle side next to the race times.

Fixes

  • A Split-S was scored as a Half Matty. A single orbit scored as nothing, then as a yaw spin. A street of posts shut the scorer off for the whole run. The training mast was not an obstacle, so nothing flown around it could count. A wall tap stuck because the direction ignored the way you had spawned.
  • Any rail was treated as a pitch, which invented flips. The lap sum in the real sim was backwards. A Maverick Loop paid 250.
  • The intro camera stayed on after the quad had left the ground, including on a throttle that never crossed the old takeoff line. Ending the shot was also undone by the next line, so the intro came back. Boot stopped waiting on a board that was asleep.

Held back

  • Powerloops, mattys, split-S, immelmanns, orbits and wall taps were being named in this week. The list is hidden as of 22 September.

22 August

22 to 30 August

New

  • Turtle. A crash can end on the props, and you flip back over from there.
  • New places: the municipal baths, The Kiln, a dressed Industrial bando, Bardwell's yard, the cement works. Blossom is back over the works road.
  • A ghost to chase, your best lap or the one before this one.
  • Credits name the people who flew it. The FPV wiki is on this website. The next flag glows on the side you pass, and the others stay dark.
  • The menus work on a phone and a tablet. A radio with no buttons can still press a row.
  • Arcade or expert while you fly. Camera out to 115 degrees. Resolution and a frame cap for a machine that is struggling. Throttle mid and expo are rows on the rates screen. They were already live in the firmware.
  • The front page asks if you want to fly, once, in the middle, then leaves the offer in the corner.

Fixes

  • Takeoff was a crash. The bang at the start line was two bugs. An upside-down gate, or a bounce off the dirt that came back upright, finished the lap. Grass is a dead thump. A hard hit flat on the base stays down. A small tap you fly out of. Turtle snaps upright when you ask.
  • First flight was swapping the track you opened for the old race field.
  • The overbridge stairs are flyable. The colliders sit on the treads. The cage is open. Gaps between posts stay gaps. Cover had been filling them. Parked cars were lifting into the air above their roofs. The pavilion is a shell, the bins are a line, a tilted dive stands on two posts, and the racing line no longer detours out to the pads.
  • The baths hopper is a cellar. The cement works is one machine, and what you hit is what is drawn. The bug chip covered the track name. The mouse could not leave a map. A mirror was blocking a gate.
  • Menus jittered because the bold label changed width under the cursor. The title note drew on top of the keep note. A failed boot says what to do. The bug report keeps what you typed. Music starts on a different track each visit.
  • Yaw was climbing, partly from one rotor. Prop wash had been turned down until it was gone, and a pilot said so. The five inch picked up the weight on the scales. A Precision tune went in for a feel report that asked for one.
  • If the board's config request failed, every link on the page broke. Credits on the board open the simulator's credits page. A pole on a plan is drawn as a pole. A fly link carries which aircraft the track is for.
