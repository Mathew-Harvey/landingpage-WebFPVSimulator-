11 August to 2 October 2026

321,396 lines of source after 53 days, from 1,139 commits across the simulator, the board and this site.

2 October

New

  • Flight paths in the builder, on the five inch and the whoop. A gate or a flag has a Flight path: Then, after the pass, Into it, before it, and on a flag Round it. There are fourteen figures, left or right where that matters, and tight, standard or wide, each with a picture: a straight with a leaning exit, a hop, a turn, a climbing turn, a descending turn, a split-S, a reverse split-S, a power loop, a corkscrew, a dive, a launch, a slalom, a figure 8 and a Matty flip. A whoop's are a seventh the size. On a touch screen the choices are under More.
  • The five inch palette has a Section tool. One click lays a straight, a sweeper, a hairpin, a chicane, esses, a step sequence, a flag slalom or a Dutch 8, with short, normal or long gaps, to the left or right, and the ghost shows it in the room.
  • Hurdles have four sizes: Nationals, which is 4 m by 1 m and what the tool puts down, 10 by 5 ft, an h-hurdle and a super hurdle. You can fly a hurdle over, skim it or, with the new Bar hurdle, go under. A Launch gate stands 15 ft up and is flown up through. A stack's spiral turns left or right, and a reverse split-S is a stack figure. The super hurdle, 20 ft by 10 ft, and the h-hurdle, a flag on a 10 ft mast with no arm, are the builder's own reading of MultiGP's rules.
  • Freestyle maps can be copied, sunk and stood on end, which one pilot's report asked for. Ctrl+D puts a copy beside the original, 1.5 m clear of it. A car copied on its own goes on along its own road, a car copied with its road rides the road's copy, a road copied alone carries no cars, and start pads are not copied. An asset can sink up to 30 m into the ground, and Base says how much is buried. Containers and the ledge can stand on end.
  • The same report asked for two assets, and they are under Industrial. A Hollow chimney is open at the top with a doorway at its foot, and its bore is 2.43 m across at the narrowest. A Wind turbine is parked: its rotor does not turn, and Rotor says where the blades stand. Scripted flights dropped down the bore, flew in through the doorway at 5 m/s and went between two blades, and the chimney wall, the tower and a blade stopped the craft.
  • The freestyle map is built in the room, as the whoop and the five inch are. It opens in 3D. A ghost stands on the roof, container or paving under the pointer, a click places it, a drag moves it with what stands on it, and a ring turns it. Roads and cars work the same way. Page Down sinks an asset that is on the ground by a quarter metre, a metre with Shift, and Page Up brings it back. The card has Stands, for flat or on end, and Copy. The 2D plan is a key away, and the map file is unchanged. The old 3D height drag is gone: a drag moves a piece across the ground, and its height is Base on the card.

Sticks

  • On Firefox for Linux, a saved stick map that is the plain game controller layout no longer overrides a Firefox radio's own. It had put the elevator stick on a switch channel, and pitch did nothing. A game controller still finds its saved map. No pilot has confirmed this yet.

Held back

  • The turbine does not turn. A turning rotor needs the physics module to move shapes about a horizontal axis, and that is not made.
  • Not built, each waiting on an answer: a banked gate, a tunnel, a sky bridge, an arch, a keyhole and a slurdle. A Dive or a Split-S laid after a gate on the ground goes under the ground, and the builder warns and does not refuse.
  • None of today's work has been flown. It was checked in a headless browser on a software renderer, with no frame rate, and on phone and tablet sizes only that way. Nobody has built a map in the room on the live site, or flown a track with these figures.

1 October

New

  • The menus are cut down. The title has eight rows: Fly, Track, Quad, Settings, How to fly, Tracks and times, About and What to fly. About holds Credits, Support, the FPV wiki, Partners and Report a bug. The pause menu is twelve items, headings included, and Graphics has left it. Settings has 24 stops, the places the cursor can land, not 33: Render scale, Frame cap, Low latency view, Predicted view, Frame pacing and the flight log are one door down, in Advanced, for when something is wrong.
  • One name for each thing. The rooms are Tracks and Maps, where they were Race and Freestyle. The room for tunes is Tune, the Betaflight key editor is Firmware bench, the shipped town is The town, where it was Freestyle city, and Upload a time is Post a time. Racing a recorded lap is Chase the record, expert and arcade physics are the Flight model, and the page that makes tracks and maps is the Builder.
  • Choosing a card in Tracks opens its sheet under the card's row: the fastest three pilots, each once, Fly it, and the rows that apply. Board tracks order by Most flown, Newest or A to Z.
  • Before you fly shows the first time you press Fly on a track in a tab, and after that Fly goes straight to the starting blocks. A press that names a track always goes straight there: a double click, Fly it, Fly this track in the builder, on the board and in Standings, and Chase the record.
  • Each aircraft keeps its own tune, PIDs, pack, weight and camera, and its own saved Your edits. Saving on the whoop used to overwrite the five inch's. The builder's Fly link put a five inch's camera back to the stock 85 degrees of view tilted 30 on every load, and so did a view of 95 or a tilt of 25 that you had chosen. Rates stay yours. The first load after this update makes the old guess once, and an edit that was already overwritten stays lost.
  • A fourth sign is painted into freestyle maps: Matt's Flooring. It is a maps only partner, so it is not on the front page, on Tracks and times or on the partners page. Its mark is found the way the others are.

The builders

  • The five inch builder builds in the room, the way the whoop's does. A new track opens in the room, from the south and framed on the track, and V switches to the plan. Lengths read in metres, and the grid has a line every 5 m.
  • New pieces: a Wall of gates that share their uprights, a Hurdle, 4 m by 1 m with a flag at each end, that you fly over, and an Up gate at 45 degrees with its lower edge 1.5 m up. Fly again, on the card, and the Fly order tool let a gate be flown twice. Gates snap a bay's width beside each other. A track with a wall of gates can be published, where it had been refused as if the wall were a cube.
  • One card sits beside any piece, and every choice on it is one click: which way it faces, Flags (none, left, right, both or top), gate size, a wall's bay, Fly over and Fly again. Round the flag lays a spiral round a gate's left or right flag, a whole turn coming down from over the header, then one pass through the opening.
  • Square is off by default. On, the first gate faces east and each next piece takes the nearest quarter turn, which is how a plan is drawn.
  • Fly order is N now, not O, which Ground logo has. The whoop builder's side drawer has a close button, and Escape closes it. Publish refuses Untitled track and Untitled map. Back to the simulator returns to the title with your work seated. Load lists each canvas's own tracks with their age, and Delete leaves an Undo in the row. Under 500 px the palette and the inspector are drawers behind Tools and Details.

Sticks

  • On a radio, pitch moves the cursor on the title and in Quad. On Rates, Tune, Firmware bench and Stick help a line says what the sticks are busy with, and the buttons choose and go back. The feel question waits for your second results and never opens by itself for a radio.
  • Stick help on Android names the channels Chrome keeps, which are 1 and 2, one of 3 and 4, and one of 5 and 6, and gives a radio recipe: in a copy of the model, clear channels 5 and 6 and give each one line with the missing stick as its source. Nobody has tried that on a phone.
  • Calibration's release step on the stick that also carries the throttle says to keep the throttle all the way down, and its hint names what it is waiting on: the throttle and its percentage, another channel by name, or an axis by the number the strip shows. A radio whose throttle springs up when you let go had made it look like a hang. That was run in the test rig only.

The board

  • The page is Tracks and times, and its tabs are Tracks and Maps. Remix in the builder is Open in the builder. Site statistics and Admin are in the footer, and the statistics have a Back to Tracks link. Pilots today is Visitors today, because it counts browsers. The masthead's Pilots counts people with a time.
  • Podiums and the sheet's table rank each pilot's best, one row a pilot, and a link under the table such as All 53 times shows every attempt. Names that differ only by case, spaces, dots, hyphens or underscores are one pilot. On a snapshot of the board one name held two podium places on 19 of 27 five inch tracks and 4 of 8 whoop tracks, and none does now. The stored times are untouched.
  • The aircraft switch covers everything under it: the track count, Built by, the tag buttons, the standings rail and Times posted. Small field and Big field are not offered on the whoop side.
  • Credits open in a tab of their own, so a flight in the simulator's tab is not reloaded. Fly this track and the chase links go to the starting blocks, as Fly this map does. The board checks again when you come back to its tab, at most once a minute, so a time you posted in the simulator shows without a reload.
  • A sheet you opened with a click closes with Close, Escape or Back, and its table no longer scrolls sideways on a phone.
  • On a phone the first track is on the first screen. At 360 by 780 the masthead went from 424 px to 78 and the first card from 942 px down to 300. Search, Built by, Order and the tags sit behind one Filter button, and the counters and the partner strip are at the foot. Fastest lap is Fastest time.
  • The list of bug reports needs an admin sign in. It had been readable by anyone who knew where it was, and so had marking a report fixed. Sending a report is unchanged.
  • The Drone Nationals 2026 qualifier is on the board: 10 gates and a closed lap of 169.4 m, credited to its designer, Wilf, and the event. It is rebuilt in the builder from the event's track diagram, for practice, and it is not the organisers' file. A first version, 12 gates and a 149 m lap, was up for 80 minutes before the corrected one replaced it. The builder's own test makes it from an empty canvas in 68 gestures.

Fixes

  • A flag on one post of a gate stood over the other post in the game, on gates flown along their facing. It follows the builder now. Two tracks that ship each had one: 2025 WA States and 2024 WA States.
  • Paused, Settings, Stick help, Escape, Escape quit the run. It no longer does.
  • A phone held upright shows five title rows, where it showed two and a half.

Held back

  • A held roll that hands the sticks back to the menu is not built on Rates and Stick help, where a held stick is the point of the screen.
  • Whether a posted time should carry your tune, your link and your flight model to the board is not decided. The note no longer promises it, and a time carries your name, lap, three lap total, ghost and weight. Tracks already on the board called Untitled stay as they are.
  • The simulator's Standings still lists every attempt, and its Tracks sheet folds only case and edge spaces in a name, where the board also folds dots, hyphens and underscores. A name spelled two ways can still appear twice there.
  • The menus were looked at in a headless browser at phone sizes and never on a real phone, and the board's pages against a snapshot. The five inch builder has been built with by a script and not by a pilot on the live site.

30 September

The whoop builder

  • You build a whoop track in the room now. Pick a gate and click the floor to place it. Press a gate and pull to move it across the floor on the inch grid, turn it with the ring at its foot, press its number to give it another place in the flying order, and type its height on the card. The plan is a camera angle: V switches between the room and the plan.
  • Every opening has a pane in RaceGOW's colours, green for the gate the lap starts on and then yellow, purple, orange and blue. Distances read in inches and millimetres as you place, the racing line is on, and a new gate takes the nearest quarter turn. An empty canvas offers Start from a RaceGOW track.
  • Help while placing. A piece pulls to a legal spot within 3 inches, and Alt turns that off. Side by side lays two or three gates 30 inches apart in one drag. Replace with swaps a gate for a stack, a tower or a horizontal gate in place. A piece that breaks a rule wears a mark, and the sentence is one hover away. The ruler measures and keeps nothing.
  • On a tablet or a phone one finger is the mouse, and two fingers move the room as one grip, sliding, spreading or twisting.
  • A piece that is flown more than once is one piece in the room: one tag with its first number and a count, and a strip of its passes along the foot. The lap bar says Passes 29 on 14 pieces for Track 8, where it said Gates 29.
  • Under More: Build sheet, to print, with where each piece stands and the pipe and fittings it takes (RaceGOW Track 1 is 15 pipes, 12 elbows and 2 tees), Picture, Copy share link, which puts the whole track in the address, and Import, which reads a track from the FPV Events designer and reports what it kept, changed and left out.
  • Six new parts, all solid when you fly them: a table, a chair, a banner, a hoop, a hex gate and a cube. Furniture turns in quarter turns. Only the hole of a hoop or a hex gate scores. A cube is a face for each side, five on the floor and six when it stands clear of it, and it moves as one.
  • The eight Season 5 tracks open as they did, and write the same lap animation.

Sticks

  • On Safari for a Mac, the no radio banner, Choose joystick, How to fly and Stick help no longer say to plug one in. They say Safari usually cannot see USB radios, and to try Chrome, Edge or Firefox. On an iPhone or iPad they say every browser there is Safari underneath, and to use a computer. That is words only: Safari still shows no radio, and why a Mac in particular does not is not proven. A bug report now says what the browser listed.
  • A pause menu opened from flight ignores the radio until roll and pitch have been still for half a second, and then roll has to be held for a fifth of a second to pick a row or go back. It had been resuming itself while a pilot's hands were still on the sticks. The title menus are unchanged, and a switch on one of the first four buttons still resumes.
  • Keys fly on a touchscreen laptop or a Chromebook. The thumb sticks had taken the sticks there and never read a key. A stick key takes the sticks and the thumb sticks go away, with the throttle carried across so nothing is punched, and a touch takes them back.

Fixes

  • Sound the browser suspended, after a device change, a sleep or a tab put away, comes back on a key, a click or a return to the tab, or by itself up to three times, and the music with it. Whether that was what the pilot who reported silence met is not known. A report now carries the audio state, so the next one will say.
  • A crash beside a wall sets you down with room. A spot with 0.347 m clear all round is chosen over the nearest clear spot when it is within a metre of it. On 1,434 test crashes 0.12 m off Hibari Yard's walls, 70 were set down with under 5 cm of room before and none now, and the set down is a median 0.50 m from the crash where it was 0.25 m. That moves 24 September's "as near as possible". It does not change what happens at the wall itself.
  • In the whoop builder, selecting a piece no longer adds an Undo step, Fit frames the track and not the whole 10 by 12 m hall, and a click in the middle of a gate picks it, where only its pipes could be picked. Import and a ?track= link keep what they replace under Load, and a whoop room you have resized keeps its size.

Held back

  • A whoop track with a table, chair, banner, hoop, hex gate or cube cannot be published yet. The board has to learn the parts first, and Publish says so before you try. The builder keeps to RaceGOW's rules: no club profiles and no free gate sizes.
  • All eight Season 5 tracks show builder warnings, from 1 to 8 each, and on Track 8 mostly gates that face away from the next. The old builder computed the same ones and listed them in a panel you had to scroll. Whether they are true of the real rooms has not been checked.
  • The room was checked in a headless browser, with touch emulated at three tablet sizes. It has not been tried on a real phone or tablet, in Firefox or in Safari, and nobody has flown a track built in it.

The Stick help screen shows where the signal is lost, in the radio, the browser or the system, and it checks with your hand on the stick before it tells you what to do. Nothing that counts changes. The scorer, the chase, a scored run's clock and your bests run as they did.
  • Manga and scoring, Off also takes the black outline off the world on Medium and High, the line Low never draws. The outlines on a few props, a gate post, a tree trunk and a vending machine, stay, and so do those on the Freestyle room's card clips. With the switch On nothing is different.

Sticks

  • A radio that Firefox calls a game controller flies the right channels. On Firefox for Linux the throttle was reading as yaw, and pitch as a switch channel.
  • Chrome on Android hands a radio four axes and drops the rest, so yaw or throttle goes missing. Stick help says so where you are stuck.

Fixes

  • A track with an absurd yaw, like 1e20, froze the tab in the builder. Big angles are brought back to the circle first.
  • A settings entry saved as null stopped the sim from starting. It reads as no settings now.
  • Every map swap left the town's instanced buffers behind. They are freed.
  • The tune's CLI download let go of its file the moment it was clicked. Safari and older Firefox start a download after the click returns, so it waits four seconds now. That fix comes from the code and has not been run in those browsers.
  • Nothing built stands in the air. FAI Turkiye 2024 had four flags about 20 m up and a gate 15 m up with nothing under them, and a pilot reported gates in the sky. Anything built more than 5 cm above what it stands on is set down when a track or map loads, and in the builder, which says what it moved. The sim says nothing. FAI Turkiye's lap is 483.8 m, where it was 505.1. 2022 AU Nationals and 2023 AU NATS 5 inch had the same fault, by up to 6.1 m.

The board

Held back

28 September

New

  • Settings has a HUD section. Its first row is Crosshairs: Off, Wings, Cross or Dot. It starts Off.

Feel

  • The default tune has a quarter more feedforward. Stick response is 125, with F at 150, 156 and 150, and three I term settings changed to take the bounce out of a stop: iterm relax cutoff 10, iterm relax RPY_INC and a yaw pidsum limit of 500. Slow to answer the stick was the most ticked box on the feel form, on 23 reports, and bounces back after a stop was ticked on nine.
  • In the sim's own stick test a flick reaches 90 percent of its roll rate 3 to 5 ms sooner. A full stick roll or pitch stop comes back about half a degree, where it came back 1.2 and 1.6, and a yaw stop at three quarter stick comes back 4.6 degrees, where it was 7.8.
  • The Tune row still says Betaflight default, with a note saying what changed. Personal bests on the default tune start again, because the record key follows the tune, and laps flown before stay under their old key. The board ranks on the clock and never sees the tune.

Screen and lag

  • The low latency canvas was never being asked for. three.js built its own context and dropped the option on every browser. The sim makes the context itself now, and Settings, Screen, Low latency view says whether your browser granted it. It is on by default.
  • Auto graphics measures. It lowers resolution a tenth after half a second of late frames and raises it a twentieth after three seconds of headroom, and on the title it can move the preset for a machine that keeps missing or keeps idling. Pick a preset by hand and Auto leaves it alone. The town and built maps follow Render scale and Auto now.
  • Predicted view draws the FPV camera where the quad will be when the frame reaches the screen. A frame is on the glass one display period after it is drawn, which at 60 Hz is 17 ms, or 11 degrees of a 670 degree per second roll. It is on by default, in Settings, Screen. It is a setting because you feel it: turn it off if a flip overshoots at the end or the picture jumps when you land.
  • Frame pacing is a Settings row: Timer with Low, Timer, always or Display, always. The timer runs the loop without waiting for the display. On one laptop at Low, key to screen fell from 80 ms to 64 at the median and from 144 ms to 80 at the 90th, over nine presses each. It draws about twice the frames, which is heat and battery, and Auto holds still while it runs. Low uses the timer by default, and so does any machine Auto puts on Low.
  • Low asks less of the GPU, and the OSD less of the page. Low draws to an 8 bit colour target where nothing reads the extra precision, drops the cloud shading it had kept after losing real shadows, and never draws more than a million pixels. The OSD updates its numbers about 15 times a second and no longer reads the layout every frame.
  • Fly, Restart and Resume go fullscreen, and Escape leaves it and pauses. Fullscreen in flight is a Settings row if you would rather not. Input to screen in Settings reads the time from a key press to the frame that first showed it. It is the browser's number, not photons. A feel report carries the flight's frame timings, and its throttle line quotes your own weight and pack: one whoop report read a hover of 39.9 percent when the pilot flew at Weight 70, where it was 32.3.

Fixes

  • On a map with no start pads you start in the open, never in a pylon. With pads, you start on them.

Held back

  • A radio that reports under about 190 times a second still flicks back a little after a small stop. The fix is in how the sim feeds the controller, and it is not made.

27 September

New

  • Three partners back WebFPV. Global Drone Solutions is the official training partner, Mantis FPV the official retail partner, and the West Coast Multirotor Club the official club partner. Their marks are on the front page and on Tracks and Times, and painted into every freestyle map. Find one in a map and you get a callout, a panel and 1000 points into the combo. Tracks and Times has a partners page, and Partner with WebFPV is on it and in the footer of the front page.
  • The menus are lettered in the manga hand: the wordmark and every room's title, with an ink frame on the panels. The quad on the results page is drawn in ink, with its arms, motors, props and a pod, and the combo tier words are drawn as strokes, so a machine with no Japanese font shows them and not empty boxes.
  • Overcast clouds are lit and inked, and tree canopies shade round instead of as a mosaic of triangles.
  • On a phone the flight lines sit in the two top corners with nothing in the middle third, and the flight chips fade after three seconds in the air and come back on a touch, on landing or on pause. The Weight slider works wherever it is shown, and pause has a Weight row.

This site

  • The front page opens as a manga page: the partners over the wordmark, the headline, and three panels for five inch racing, freestyle and whoop racing. Scroll and the page opens into the film. The town is gone from the film, so the lap goes straight to the map builder. The whoop room in the film is the sakura room. The picture that shows when you paste a link to this site is the first screen.
  • The wiki was checked against Betaflight's source. It said that without airmode the motors sit at idle and you have no control, and that the angle limit is about thirty degrees. The mixer fits the throttle either way, and the limit is 60. Five settings that were labelled Works here do nothing in this build, and are Stored, not used. The 103 settings that had no description have one, and Back in the wiki opens the article.

Sticks

  • A game controller flies its own sticks in your stick mode. Mode 1 or Mode 2 now reaches a pad, so a controller that had throttle on the right stick has it on the left in Mode 2.
  • The help row for a radio whose throttle reads as yaw comes about four seconds after you leave the throttle alone. It had come up to five times late.

The board

  • A time goes up at any weight, and says so. The board keeps the weight you flew at and prints "Weight 60%" beside your name on any row that is not 100. Ranking is still on the clock.

Fixes

  • Firefox would not start the sim from the evening of the 25th. The page said "The specifier three was a bare specifier". The loader change of the 25th wrote its import map after Cloudflare's own module script had started, and Firefox refuses a map that late. The map is written before the body now, and Firefox starts the sim again.
  • Crashes are judged on every physics step, not once a frame. A 60 Hz laptop and a 144 Hz monitor call the same hit the same way, and you are set down where you hit.
  • A crash is never set down where the craft cannot take off. One had put a craft under the crane's bottom bar. The crane's foot is solid where it is drawn.
  • Tracks: a card is chosen where you click it, and a double click flies it. The page used to scroll after the first click and leave Fly below the fold.
  • The drift coupe and the compact coupe were redrawn to read as the cars they are.
  • The same opening twice in a row is two passes, not one. On 2022 AU Nationals the lap counts only when 32 to 36 is flown, the loop is flown, and 32 to 36 is flown again.

Held back

  • Finding the SubTwoFifty mark is no longer an achievement, and the pill is gone from the map cards. The mark stays painted on every map.

26 September

New

  • Cars drive the roads. The builder has a road tool and vehicles, and Play in the preview drives them. A car is a solid that follows its road at the speed its curves allow, and you can hit it.
  • Chase a car. The Tail meter builds while you hold its bumper and banks when you peel off. Hitting a car loses the tail and pays nothing.
  • Hibari Yard has a loop of two lane road, 7.5 m wide, where a drift coupe drives with a compact blue coupe chasing it. Every car was redrawn, with a face of its own for each kind. Hibari Yard Tandem is the showpiece map: a drift course with two drift coupes in tandem. It is on the board.
  • Freestyle maps score the line you fly. Named gaps pay when you go through them. Skims along a wall or a roof, threads between two solids, unders and low passes are close calls. All of it goes into one combo, and a crash drops the whole combo. A low pass pays its points and buys no multiplier. Trick names stay behind the Scoring row in the Freestyle room, which starts on Lines only.
  • The freestyle score is lettered in a manga hand, small and out of the middle of the picture. Past 72 km/h ink strokes gather at the edges and lean toward where you are heading, and a crash freezes for a beat in ink. After a scored run the results are a manga page with a share card. Clean FPV and Impact frame are Settings rows.
  • Race tracks have a fourth lap count, Practice. It has no end, and nothing you fly in it goes to the board. Lap times are called out loud, in your browser's own voice, and the call follows Volume in Settings.
  • Support is a row on the title and pause menus and opens Patreon. Its note gives the tiers: Keep the lights on is $3, Hosting + runway is $8 and Build the sim is $20, US dollars a month. The price came down. A click on Support is counted once, with nothing about who clicked, and nothing is sent if your browser sends Global Privacy Control.
  • The Freestyle room lists the board's ten newest maps and pictures the world you are flying. Pause has Back to the track builder on a track or map you built, and Fly this track goes straight to the starting blocks.

The whoop

  • The whoop room is recoloured in the sakura theme, lit like a room and not a sunset, with the slap pack stickers as posters and banners on its walls. The logos painted on the floor show on the mat.
  • The whoop's OSD has no speed readout. The number was a five inch's ground speed printed over a room built 3.4 times life size.
  • The whoop builder lets you delete any side of a gate, one at a time, for a virtual gate that keeps its trigger area, and drag any part of the flight path in 3D to bend it. A pole is drawn as a pole and not a flag, and the square swings on its pole the way it scores.

The board

  • RaceGOW rooms rank on the fastest three consecutive laps, and the best single lap sits in the next column. A run that never put three clean laps together is stored and stays off the sheet. The simulator's standings follow the same rule.
  • The board refuses laps nobody can fly: none under a quarter of a second, and none faster than the track's length at 50 m/s. Three impossible records came off, a 0.20 s lap round Flags and cones and laps of 10 and 15 ms on Orbit.

This site

  • The front page has a freestyle chapter. The map builder puts a yard together, then the film chases a drift tandem round it. A menu after the build jumps to any chapter, and each chapter has a picture of its world as it is now.

Fixes

  • On a plain gate the race OSD read "GATE 1 OF 12, SPLIT-S, LEVEL 1". It reads "GATE 1 OF 12" now. The Air slot is airtime from takeoff, not the lap clock, which ran on the pads.
  • The Weight card retires by itself: on your first landing or crash, after 8 seconds in the air, or on your first stick move in the air. The Weight slider fades while you fly and comes back when you land or pause.
  • The OSD is inked, so PACK, height, THROTTLE, SCORE, AIR and the stick captions read over pale concrete and overcast. Values that were cut off with dots read whole, like Camera angle 30 degrees and Rates as Actual 670/670/670.
  • In the builder the racing line no longer leaves a gate and doubles back through it to reach the next. Labels can be switched off while building, the top bar wraps instead of hiding Undo, Redo and the views, and the lap export works when the builder's scripts are cached.
  • After a station counts, the next one needs a little flying first, half a metre on the field and less in a room, so on Orbit rocking on the flag no longer counts laps. The builder warns when two stations in a row stand closer than that.
  • The results menu no longer covers the run's rows. A plain roll around a rail scores as a Roll or nothing, never a Maverick Loop. A crash on a road puts you on the verge, a little over 4 m from its middle, with the traffic going past. Drift smoke near the eye thins to white and not to ink.

Held back

  • Screentone, ink dots in the darkest band at High, is built and off, because the dots swim on the glass.
  • The Trick list is still off the title and the Freestyle room.

25 September

New

  • You can build a freestyle map. Open the track builder, pick Freestyle, lay a map out from a library of drawn pieces, bandos, cranes and named gaps among them, and press Fly this map. It starts you in the air, not on a menu. Until you build one, Your map is Hibari Yard, a starter yard.
  • The screen where you pick what to fly has a fourth card, Map builder. The builder asks what you are building, five inch, whoop or freestyle, with a picture of each.
  • Freestyle maps go on the board. Publish from the builder and the board has a Freestyle maps tab beside the tracks. Fly a map from there, or remix it in the builder. Site statistics moved next to Admin to make room.
  • Every freestyle map carries the SubTwoFifty mark, painted on a wall you can see from the pads. Finding it is an achievement.
  • A link to a track or a map shows that track or map as its picture when you paste it somewhere that draws a preview.
  • Site statistics count which site or link a visit came from: the name of the site that sent you, or a ref tag in the link, and never the full address of the page. A tag that is not on the fixed list is counted as other. The privacy panel on the board says so.

This site

  • The front page has a short FAQ and a new share picture, and it no longer overlaps itself on a short laptop screen. Every wiki article has its own address and its own preview. Old links with a # still work.

Fixes

  • A finger can scroll the menus on an iPhone, and a vertical swipe on the quad picture scrolls the page.
  • A town that failed to load no longer leaves you in a room with nothing to press.
  • A slow belly first tap on a wall is not a crash. The sim read the wall's direction in the wrong frame, so on some maps a tap counted when it should not have.
  • Tags survive a republish. A track lost its tags whenever it was republished without re-ticking them, and whenever the pilot renamed themselves. Publishing goes up as a copy when the board already has the id.
  • Every page loads the scripts of the deploy it was served from, so a page and its scripts come from the same deploy. The Publish button had gone missing for one pilot that way.

24 September

New

  • The solid world is in the physics. Walls, roofs, gates, trees and the train are solved in the same 1 ms step as the ground, so a roof lands, slides and settles like the street. Props flex and skate along a face instead of levering the frame, and the camera is kept off the glass so it cannot end up inside a wall.
  • A crash is a reset, at once. A hit of 4 m/s or more that does not land on your belly sets you down nearby: "Crashed, set down nearby. R restarts the run." You no longer hang on a wall or wait on your back.
  • A crashed quad falls flat and ends on its belly or its back. It could stand on a corner at whatever angle it hit. A recovery sets you down on the ground or a roof top, never in the air, which had put a whoop on a room's ceiling.
  • The whole wiki is rewritten for a Year 10 reader: all 36 articles, every settings page, the figure text and the glossary. Sections are The idea, How it works and In this simulator, and the chips say Works here, Off at 1 kHz, Stored, not used, Not simulated and Configurator only. Every claim was checked against the simulator's code, which had moved on: two aircraft, a 0.71 kg five inch, contact inside the physics module, and turtle on T. The ground page follows tumble flat.

The whoop

  • The whoop is heavier by default. Weight 100 on a whoop now flies what 125 flew, because a pilot flying the same track in another sim had to take it to 120 to 130. The slider tops out at 120 on the whoop, and whoop bests carry over. The five inch is unchanged.
  • The whoop reads 1S and 4.2 V. It said 25 V at the start of a race. Only the label changed, not the physics, and its screens stop calling it a five inch.

Sticks

  • On the keyboard, letting go of the throttle keys holds height. The throttle springs to the hover for your cap, aircraft, weight and pack, where it had sprung to a fixed low value and dropped the quad. M switches between Angle and Acro.
  • Settings, Sticks, Restart switch, with a radio: choose it, flip any AUX or press any button, and from then on flipping it does what R does. It is kept per radio.
  • When a browser reads your radio's throttle as yaw, the title says so. The joystick picker draws what the page will fly, not a gamepad's axes.

The board

  • The Statistics tab reads plainer, and its sponsor plate is gone. The all time strip drops named pilots and pilots back another day.

Fixes

  • The first flight on the new walls found three faults. The town sat at "still loading" for 47 seconds, so its modules are preloaded now. A quad stuck on a wall face, or on its back, is set down nearby.
  • A feel report carries what the flight measured, not what the pause screen shows.

22 September

16 to 22 September

New

  • Patreon is under the WebFPV name on the title screen, and in the bar on this site. Memberships start at $3 USD a month.
  • There is a weight slider on the flight screen. Right is heavier, so the quad drops when you chop the throttle. Left is lighter and it hangs. Normal sits at ninety percent of full Sinky, which is where flying it to the stop felt about right, with room either side. The old slider changed drag, how far the quad carries, and a lap line could print "air at undefined percent".
  • Rates are saved in this browser, throttle cap included, and you can keep more than one set. Open them from pause. Changing rates in the air used to restart the run and drop you on the ground.
  • Stick mode is Mode 1 and Mode 2, on a radio, a game controller, or the keyboard.
  • Tracks and Times has a Statistics tab. Crashes are in it. A crashflip is left out of the flight time.

The town

  • Gaps that looked open were solid. A ceiling was missing between two buildings, a hip roof had no peak, and a roof deck you could see was a lid.
  • The bridge deck had a slot in it. The canal was solid. The mountains were hollow and the bore had no roof. Four bushes were sitting in the air on a crest. The cafe tables were 1.6 m past the rail.
  • Hit a wall and you bounced between two spots until you restarted. The props were pushing you into the face.

Sticks

  • A game controller's throttle springs back to centre. The sim read that spring as a punch, and the quad took off by itself.
  • The calibration wizard says what it is waiting for. You can finish it with the throttle held down, and a four-axis radio can finish it. A three-position switch is no longer asked to return to a centre it does not have. A gimbal that rests a little off centre keeps that rest, so the quad does not creep with the sticks let go.
  • One channel ran backwards, the sticks were on the wrong hands, and a stick stayed down after you let go. The camera angle slider was moving the quad.

Fixes

  • The settings room is called Settings. The menus were sending you to a calibrate screen that was not there.
  • Menu rows slid out from under the pointer. The help note was changing the height of the row.
  • A frozen picture says what it is waiting on.
  • The board's crash count read zero. It only heard the clip-through catch, and missed a real hit. Race and freestyle now use the same line, and a turtle after that hit is not counted again. A visit's country was coming through as Unknown.
  • The empty-board sentence was showing up when the board had failed to load, tracks and all.
  • A rate preset with no id showed in the list and could not be loaded or deleted.
  • A feel report now records the sticks as well as the tune. Five reports that said twitchy, stiff, floppy or soft were two phones, a keyboard whose held key only reached about a third of the stick, and a RadioMaster Boxer.
  • Tune is one list, in the Quad room. Two old tunes came off it, and the aircraft picker is in that room.
  • The flight music lost three tracks and gained five. The wiki was saying the same thing twice on a page.

Held back
